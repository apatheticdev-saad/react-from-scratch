## 🚀 What's this?

I've started learning **React from scratch** — and this repo is where I document everything.

No bootcamp. No fixed schedule. Just me, the fundamentals, and real projects. 💪

My approach is simple — whenever I understand a concept well enough to explain it myself, I push it here. That might come from the official docs 📄, a YouTube video 🎥, someone's blog ✍️, or just experimenting on my own 🧪. Whatever helps the concept **click**, that's what I'll use.

The focus is on **understanding**, not completion.
> 💡 I'd rather truly get one thing than skim through ten.

---

## 🧠 How I'm Learning

- 🏗️ **Project-first** — I learn by building, not just reading
- 🔩 **Fundamentals matter** — I won't skip the basics to get to the cool stuff
- 🖊️ **My own words** — every note here is written by me, not copied
- ⏳ **No pressure on timeline** — I go when I understand, not when the calendar says so
